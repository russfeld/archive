Google Android

  • (Mostly) Open Source
  • Many different flavors
  • Able to install apps from any source
  • ~80% market share
  • Sandboxed permission system

Apple iOS

  • Proprietary Software
  • Consistent across devices
  • Gated app store
  • ~15% market share

Blackberry

  • Proprietary Software
  • Systems (usually) cannot be upgraded
  • Can run Android applications
  • ~3-5% market share

Windows Phone

  • Proprietary Software
  • Systems (usually) cannot be upgraded
  • Able to install apps from any source
  • ~3-5% market share

Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

  • User want to bring their personal devices to work
  • They want network access, email access, support, advice, training, etc.
  • Upsides?
  • Downsides?
  • Which Platform?

BYOD Upsides

  • Users get newer devices
  • Company doesn't pay for them
  • Work anytime, anywhere
  • Users are happier
  • Users self-teach new technology
  • Less to carry

BYOD Downsides

  • IT has to support more devices
  • Data can be easily lost/stolen
  • Users may not have enough money
  • No consistency between devices
  • More distractions at work
  • Hard to get away from work at home
  • IT costs may actually go up!
