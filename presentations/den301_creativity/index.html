Building Creativity

Creativity

noun: "A phenomenon whereby something new and in some way valuable is created" Wikipedia

Research: Measuring Creativity

  • Creativity Quotient (CQ) - unsuccessful
  • Divergence Testing - find all possible answers instead of converging on the right one
  • Psychometric Study - tests to measure creativity such as unusual uses and uncommon responses to words

Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking

  • Fluency - The total number of relevant ideas generated in response to a stimulus
  • Originality - The statistical rarity of the responses
  • Elaboration - The amount of detail in the responses

Source: Wikipedia

Vocabulary Game

  1. Pick a letter
  2. Pick a category
  3. Come up with as MANY items starting with the letter and pertaining to the category as you can

Source: Byrdseed

Vocabulary Game

  • Fluency: How many did you get?
  • Originality: How many are unique?
  • Elaboration: Choose 1, write a detailed paragraph in 3 minutes

Alternative Uses Game

  1. Choose a random object nearby
  2. Think of as many alternative uses of that object as possible in 3 minutes

Source: Byrdseed

Alternative Uses Game

  • Fluency: How many did you get?
  • Originality: How many are unique?
  • Elaboration: Choose 1, tell us in great detail why it is a good use in 90 seconds

Other Ideas

  • Start with shapes and combine them
  • Use blocks, tangrams, play-doh, sand to build
  • Tear paper into a design
  • Change a coloring book page to be something different

Building Creativity

  • Have a purpose or intent
  • Reward curiosity and exploration
  • Provide opportunity for choice and discovery independently (alone)
  • Encourage confidence in risk-taking
  • Redefine problems (and solutions)
  • Start with many ideas and expand on them without fear of failure

Hindering Creativity

  • "That won't work"
  • "We've never done it that way"
  • "That idea isn't relevant"
  • "We don't know how to do that"
  • "I don't understand your idea"
  • "Let's find a solution everyone agrees on first"
  • Only listening to the loudest people
