Using Social Media Safely

Social Media DO

  • Lock down your privacy settings
  • Think before your post
  • Use Long and Strong passwords
  • Communicate with friends and family
  • Share pictures (selectively)
  • Encourage citizenship and healthy living

Social Media DON'T

  • Post your personal information
  • Post photos on a public site
  • Talk negatively about someone
  • Share someone else's secrets
  • Share your location online
  • Make threats of violence (even in jest)
  • Post inappropriate content

Source: LiamOliverTyler via Twitter

Source: AmpliFound

Cyber Bullying

  • 52% of youth report being cyber bullied
  • 95% of teens who witness cyber bullying ignore the behavior
  • All genders are equally likely to be bullied (and to bully others)
  • Cyber bullying affects all races
  • Bullies often imitate other friends to gain inside information
  • Can cause depression, low self-esteem, low grades, and even suicide

Source: HelpGuide.org

4-H Ambassador → Online Ambassador

  • Look out for your fellow students
  • Alert an adult if you see cyber bullying
  • BE RELENTLESS! Keep reporting
  • Save/Screenshot the messages
  • Talk privately with the victim
  • Ignore the bully (don't feed trolls)
  • Don't blame anyone (not your job)
  • Meet up IRL instead (Ice Cream!)
